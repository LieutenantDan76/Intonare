Intonare

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

Short version: Intonare does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your microphone is used only for pitch detection, locally on your device, and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The app offers one optional purchase, processed through Google Play and RevenueCat, which involves only anonymous purchase data and no personal information. Nothing is ever shared with third parties for advertising.

Who We Are

Intonare is a music education app developed by LieutenantDan76, an independent developer based in Italy. The app is available on Android (Google Play Store), iOS (Apple App Store), and as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

Data We Do Not Collect

Intonare does not collect, process, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information of any kind (name, email, age, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or behavioural telemetry
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Audio recordings
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to external servers
  • Payment card details or billing information (handled entirely by Google Play; see Purchases below)

Microphone Access

The app requests access to your device's microphone solely to enable its pitch detection and tuner features. Audio from your microphone is:

  • Processed locally on your device in real time using the Web Audio API
  • Never recorded to a file or stored in any form
  • Never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party

Microphone access is only active while you are actively using a feature that requires it (such as the tuner or pitch exercises). You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your device's system settings without affecting other features of the app.

Internet Access

Intonare requires an internet connection solely to load audio instrument samples on first use. These samples are fetched from gleitz's open-source MIDI soundfonts, a public repository of royalty-free audio files. No personal data is transmitted during these requests — they are equivalent to loading an image from a website. Once loaded, samples may be cached by the browser for offline use.

Local Storage

Intonare stores a small amount of data locally on your device, including your settings preferences, exercise history, and progress statistics. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device and is never uploaded or synced
  • Is never shared with the developer or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing app data in your device settings

Purchases

Intonare offers a single optional one-time purchase that unlocks the app's premium features. This purchase is handled through Google Play's billing system and a service called RevenueCat, which manages your unlock so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new device.

When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase status. It does not receive your name, email address, or payment card details. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play and is never seen by the developer or by RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes this data solely on the developer's behalf to manage your unlock, and the data is encrypted in transit.

If you never make a purchase, no purchase data is generated or collected. If you have made a purchase and would like the associated purchase data deleted, contact the developer (see Contact below) and it will be removed.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

Intonare does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks. There are no ads in the app. The only third-party services involved are Google Play's billing system and RevenueCat, used solely to process the optional in-app purchase described under Purchases above. No data is shared with any third party for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose whatsoever.

Aside from processing purchases, the only external network requests the app makes are to load audio sample files, as described under Internet Access above.

Children's Privacy

Intonare does not collect any personal data from any users, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The optional purchase described under Purchases involves only anonymous data with no personal information attached. The app is safe for use by learners of all ages. Because no personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored off-device, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, or comparable laws.

The microphone is used only for real-time, on-device pitch detection and is never recorded or transmitted (see Microphone Access above). Because no personal data is collected, no special parental consent mechanisms are required or implemented.

Your Rights (GDPR / EU Users)

Because Intonare collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. All data associated with your use of the app exists only on your own device and is entirely under your control.

If you have questions or concerns about data handling, you may contact the developer directly (see below).

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you rights regarding personal information that a business collects about you. Because Intonare collects no personal information, the following all hold true:

  • We collect no categories of personal information.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and never have.
  • We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what is permitted without a right to limit.

There is therefore nothing to access, delete, correct, or opt out of. You will never be discriminated against for exercising any privacy right, as there is no data to act upon.

Changes to This Policy

Given the nature of the app, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely unless significant new features are added. Users are not automatically notified of policy updates; we encourage you to check this page periodically if you have concerns.
