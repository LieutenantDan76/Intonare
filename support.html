Support

Help for the Intonare app on iPhone and Android

Need help? Email Intonare.dev@gmail.com. A real person reads every message, usually within a few days.

Common questions

I bought Pro and it is locked again.
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Restoring is free and works on a new phone too.

The tuner does not hear me.
The tuner needs microphone permission. Open your phone's settings, find Intonare, and turn on the microphone. The sound is analyzed on your phone and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Fonts and sounds are built into the app. The only parts that need a connection are the optional online quiz categories.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Use Back Up Progress in the app's settings to save a file, then restore it on the new phone. Your streaks, scores and settings come along.

Can I change the language?
Intonare is available in English and Italian. You can switch in the app's settings.

Reporting a problem

The fastest fix comes from a clear report. Please include:

  • Your phone model and its system version
  • What you tapped and what you expected to happen
  • What happened instead, with a screenshot or short screen recording if you can

Bugs and ideas are also welcome on the project page on GitHub.
